Flight Archive should use ICAO-named airline logos from:

```text
airline-logos/flightaware_logos/{ICAO}.png
```

For example, Cathay Pacific (`CPA`) is available at:

```text
airline-logos/flightaware_logos/CPA.png
```

The airline and operator marks remain the property of their respective owners.
See the upstream `README.md` for its disclaimer and intended identification use.
